Hibiki

What resonates with you. Find people and posts on Nostr beyond your circle, tuned to your own taste and humour, with spam filtered out by nodes that cooperate.

Beyond your follows

Discovers "taste neighbours": strangers who enjoy the same things you do, not just friends of friends.

Your taste stays yours

Private signals are sealed in your browser. Your node only ever sees public data.

You steer it

Sliders for close or far, familiar or new, serious or playful. Every post shows why it's there.

Nodes against spam

Torii nodes share signed, advisory scores. No single peer can hide anything from you.

Roadmap

  1. Preview card — you are here
  2. Circle feed — follows, friends of friends, spam gate, explanations
  3. Private taste — taste neighbours, sliders, exploration
  4. Optional local embeddings — only if your host has room
  5. Signed node scores — readable by any NIP-85 client
  6. Peer cooperation — weighted, decaying, optional FIPS
  7. Mount anywhere — Continuum, Quest and Torii DE
